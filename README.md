## About Me

I am an undergraduate student at **Peking University**, working at the intersection of **robotics**, **3D vision**, **robot learning**, and **generative modeling**.

My current interests focus on building **simple, scalable, and physically meaningful models** for hand-object interaction and robot manipulation. I am especially interested in:

- **Dexterous grasp generation and tracking**
- **Hand-object interaction modeling**
- **3D vision and real-to-sim pipelines**
- **Vision / tactile representation learning**
- **World models for robot learning**
- **Flow Matching, diffusion models, and generative policies**
- **Reinforcement learning and sim-to-real transfer**

I enjoy thinking about how to make model design cleaner and more scalable, while still preserving useful geometric and physical structure.
